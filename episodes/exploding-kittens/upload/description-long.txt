I Simulated 10 Billion Games of Exploding Kittens. Here's How to Win.

The best player at an Exploding Kittens table wins three times as often as the worst one, with the same cards. I simulated more than 10 billion games to find out why.

I built the game from the official rulebook (original edition, 56 cards), made bots that only know what a real player would know, and tested one habit at a time, 100 million games per test. It comes down to five mistakes most tables make.

Everything in this video, the simulation, the animation and the charts, is made with code. Not affiliated with Exploding Kittens; card designs are my own.
