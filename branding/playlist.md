**Title:** Board & Card Games, Simulated

**Description:**
How do you actually win the games everyone plays? I build each game in code, give bots different
strategies, and let them play millions of games against each other. Then I tell you which habits win,
which ones lose, and which "obvious" moves are secretly bad.

New game every week. Exploding Kittens, Uno, Connect 4, Shut the Box, Battleship and more.
