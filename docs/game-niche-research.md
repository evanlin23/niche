# Game-strategy niche: research (YouTube search scan, 2026-11-04)

Method: top 15 YouTube results for "best <game> strategy" across 50 games (yt-dlp, logged-out,
US). Demand = combined views of those results; "sim/data" = titles about simulation, maths,
"tested on N games", "solved", etc. Raw data: game-search-scan-2026-11-04.txt.
Limitation: view counts of search results, not search volume; one snapshot.

## Key findings
1. The format works: Perfect Play (new channel, ~Oct 2026) made Monopoly 2.7M, Catan 306K
   (4 days), Risk 191K, Minesweeper 45K, from 4 uploads.
2. Copycats of the exact title formula ("The Best X Strategy, Tested on N Million Games")
   already exist and mostly get 0–6K views: Jacab Thomas (Clue, Sorry!, Battleship), Million
   Games and Dice Lab (Uno), Eyaci Ecin (Solitaire), Chessiron (RPS), The Odds Engine
   (Battleship), Rulebook Math (Farkle), Solved by Code (Mancala). Copying the formula is not enough.
3. Big hits are specific questions with strong packaging: Mark Rober "Guess Who 96% win record"
   31M, "I Solved Every Board Game" 19.5M; Ballpark Figures "I Solved Wordle" 2.1M and
   "I Solved Yahtzee" 1.1M; 2swap "I Solved Connect 4" 1.7M; Ioana Roman Catan 393K.
